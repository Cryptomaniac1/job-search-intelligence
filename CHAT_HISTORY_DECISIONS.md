# Historical Decisions

## LinkedIn Strategy

Prefer jobs with fewer than 100 applicants.

Browser extension should surface low-competition opportunities.

---

## Recruiter CRM

Every recruiter interaction becomes part of a long-term relationship history.

---

## Email Philosophy

Emails are the source of truth.

Calendar supplements emails but does not replace them.

## Analytics

The system should compute

- recruiter response rate

- interview conversion

- company conversion

- role conversion

- application aging

- recruiter activity

---

## AI Usage

AI should summarize.

AI should recommend.

AI should never overwrite historical data automatically.

---

## Goal

Create a permanent career operating system rather than another job tracker.
